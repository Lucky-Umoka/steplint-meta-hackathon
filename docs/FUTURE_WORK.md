## Definition of the current core gate

A learner selects that local guide and is guided through camera setup, Ready/observed automatic start, correct/wrong/uncertain actions, recovery and goal completion. The evaluator remains the rule authority. Store the revision/method and evidence needed for references. Record real-model outcomes separately from mocked tests, replayed cameras and physical-camera acceptance. No claim of universal accuracy.
